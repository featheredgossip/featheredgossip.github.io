About Feathered Gossip

Birds with opinions. Feathered Gossip is a small, independent shop of funny bird designs and a blog for people who love birds, from brand-new backyard birders to people with a well-worn field guide.

What we write about

We write about the birds people actually see: garden birds of Britain and Europe, backyard birds of North America and a few birders' favourites from further away. You will find identification guides, surprising facts, the science behind odd bird behaviour, tips for making your garden or backyard bird-friendly, and the occasional gift guide.

How our articles are made

Every article starts with research: the species' Wikipedia article (which is built on the ornithological literature) and, where available, trusted bird organisations such as the RSPB, the BTO, the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and the National Audubon Society. We draft articles with the help of AI writing tools, using only the researched facts, and then run a separate fact-check before anything is published. Each article lists the sources it is based on.

We are bird lovers, not a scientific institution. If you spot a mistake, we really want to know and we will fix it quickly.

Our designs

Our bird designs are sold through our shop on Redbubble, which prints and ships every order. When an article mentions our stickers, T-shirts or hoodies, the link goes to our own shop. We do not take money from anyone else for mentions or links.
