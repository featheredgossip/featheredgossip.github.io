Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This website is a static blog. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not run ads, and we do not set our own cookies or use analytics or tracking scripts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are downloaded. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Shop links

Links to our shop take you to Redbubble, which has its own privacy policy and handles all orders and payments.
